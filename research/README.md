# Initial research corpus

This is the starting research set for the Behavioral Simulation method. The implementation work should extract operational principles from the papers rather than merely summarize them.

## 1. Social Simulacra: Creating Populated Prototypes for Social Computing Systems

Joon Sung Park, Lindsay Popowski, Carrie J. Cai, Meredith Ringel Morris, Percy Liang, Michael S. Bernstein. UIST 2022.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3526113.3545616
- User-provided PDF: https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3526113.3545616
- Primary contribution to extract: populated prototypes for exploring emergent social behavior before real deployment.

## 2. Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior

Joon Sung Park, Joseph O'Brien, Carrie Jun Cai, Meredith Ringel Morris, Percy Liang, Michael S. Bernstein. UIST 2023.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3586183.3606763
- User-provided PDF: https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3586183.3606763
- Primary contribution to extract: memory, retrieval, reflection, and planning architecture for persistent believable agents and emergent multi-agent behavior.

## 3. Generative Agent Simulations of 1,000 People

Joon Sung Park, Carolyn Q. Zou, Aaron Shaw, Benjamin Mako Hill, Carrie Cai, Meredith Ringel Morris, Robb Willer, Percy Liang, Michael S. Bernstein. 2024.

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10109
- User-provided PDF mirror: https://antephase.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Generative-Agent-Simulations-of-1000-People-archive.pdf
- Primary contribution to extract: grounding agents in extensive qualitative interviews with real individuals and evaluating how well agents reproduce those individuals' responses.

## 4. Finetuning LLMs for Human Behavior Prediction in Social Science Experiments

Akaash Kolluri, Shengguang Wu, Joon Sung Park, Michael S. Bernstein. EMNLP 2025.

- ACL Anthology: https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1530/
- PDF: https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1530.pdf
- Primary contribution to extract: population-level behavioral prediction from large-scale real experimental response data, out-of-study and out-of-condition generalization, distributional evaluation, and bias measurement.

## 5. On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models

Rishi Bommasani et al. 2021.

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.07258
- User-provided PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.07258/pdf

## Research-extraction rule

For each source, the later methodology pass should record:

- the research question;
- study design and population;
- model inputs and architecture;
- target behaviors/outcomes;
- validation design;
- evaluation metrics;
- strongest supported claims;
- limitations and failure modes;
- privacy/ethics constraints;
- what can be operationalized in a reusable agent skill;
- what must remain a caveat rather than a feature.
